About

I help organisations through their worst day, and find out exactly what happened.

For more than eight years I've led the response when organisations are hit by ransomware, data breaches, insider threats and business email compromise: finding how attackers got in, how far they reached, and how to recover.

I turn complex evidence into clear answers for executives, legal teams and insurers. These days I'm especially focused on how AI can sharpen detection and speed up investigations.
